# Claim 2 source audit

Theorem 3.11 states that, under Assumptions 3.1 and 3.10, every eigenvector of the clean sample covariance has contaminated residual `O_p(n^-1/2)`. Assumption 3.10 requires only convergence of the empirical spectral distribution of `XX^T/n` to a compactly supported law, with `d/n -> c`. It does not require centered columns, iid entries, or delocalized right singular vectors. Assumption 3.1 permits a finite-rank Bernoulli mean shift independent of `X`.
